Introduction

Social Uploader ("the app") prepares photos so they keep their quality when you post them to social media such as Facebook and Instagram. The app is published by Mid-May ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app and the services it relies on handle. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

Effective date: November 18, 2026.

Your photos

The photos you choose are processed entirely on your device. We do not upload them to any server and we cannot see them. The app has no account or sign-in.

  • You select photos with Android's system photo picker, or share them to the app from another app, so the app only receives the images you pick.
  • Processed copies are kept temporarily in the app's private storage and are cleared when you start a new batch.
  • When you tap Share, the processed photos go to the app you choose (for example Facebook or Instagram), which handles them under its own privacy policy.
  • When you tap Save, a copy is written to the Pictures/SocialUploader folder on your device.

Information collected by third-party services

We do not run our own servers or collect personal data ourselves. The app uses the services below, which may collect data automatically under their own privacy policies.

Data on your device and permissions

The app stores a few settings on your device only: your language choice, whether Pro is unlocked, and a counter that decides how often a full-screen ad appears. This information is not sent to us.

The app uses internet access and network status so ads, crash reports, settings and purchases work. Because of AdMob, it also declares the Advertising ID permission. The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location or general storage.

Your choices and consent

  • Ad consent: where the law requires it, the app shows a Google consent form before any ads load. If you decline, ads are not requested. You can reset your choice by clearing the app's data.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset it or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings, usually under Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Remove ads: unlocking Pro removes all ads.
  • Crash reports: these are collected automatically and cannot be switched off inside the app. You can stop them by uninstalling the app or by contacting us.
  • Language: you can change the app language on the home screen or in your device settings.

Retention, security and your rights

Retention. We do not keep personal data. Data held by Google and Firebase is kept for the periods set in their own policies.

Security. Your photos never leave your device unless you share them yourself. The third-party services above transmit data using encryption.

International transfers. Google may process data in the countries where it operates.

Children. The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help.

Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its processing. We hold no personal data ourselves, so requests about data handled by Google or Firebase are best sent to Google. For anything else, contact us.
